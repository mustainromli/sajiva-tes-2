Frequently Asked Questions (Cardamom Export)

What makes Indonesian White Cardamom (Amomum compactum) unique?

Indonesian White Cardamom (Kapulaga Jawa) is distinct from green Guatemalan or Indian cardamom. Its spherical, cream-white dried pods possess high cineole concentrations with warm camphor and refreshing floral notes, heavily favored for spice blends, herbal formulations, traditional medicines, and essential oil distillation.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and sample policy?

Our standard export minimum order quantity (MOQ) is 1 Metric Ton (1,000 kg). For prospective buyers needing quality evaluation, we offer commercial sample batches (500g - 2kg) shipped via DHL/FedEx worldwide with complete lab parameters.

Which Incoterms and ports of loading do you provide?

We regularly operate on FOB Port of Tanjung Priok (Jakarta) or FOB Port of Makassar (Sulawesi), as well as CIF to all major international sea and air destinations. Our logistics department coordinates full container loads (FCL) and consolidated shipments (LCL).
